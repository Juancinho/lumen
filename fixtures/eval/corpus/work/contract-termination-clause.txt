Section 14 - Termination

14.1 Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience by giving the other party
thirty (30) days' prior written notice.
14.2 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party commits a material breach
that is not remedied within fifteen (15) days of written notice describing the breach.
14.3 Upon termination the customer shall pay all fees accrued up to the termination date,
and each party shall return or destroy the other's confidential information.
