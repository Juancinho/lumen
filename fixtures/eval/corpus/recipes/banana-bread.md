# Banana bread

Makes one loaf.

- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 75 g melted butter
- 150 g sugar, 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda, a pinch of salt, 190 g flour

Mix the wet ingredients, fold in the dry ones, pour into a buttered tin and bake at
175 C for about 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
